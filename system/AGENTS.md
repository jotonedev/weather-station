# Rules

## Testing

- Use clear, descriptive variable names (e.g. `readings`, `wantStored`), avoid single-letter variables.

## Writing style

- Do not use em dashes
- Try to not use colon and semicolon
- Do not chain adjectives
- Do not repeat information said later or before
- Do not use "It's not X, it's Y"
- Be clear and concise
